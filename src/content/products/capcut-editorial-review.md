## Why it is in our workflow

CapCut is the speed lane. When a clip is generated and quality-checked, the remaining jobs — trim, captions, a subscribe overlay, a sound effect on the hook moment, export in 9:16 — need minutes, not an afternoon. That is the job CapCut does well.

## Where we switch tools

For cinematic colour, complex composites or final mastering of a long compilation, we move to a full editor such as DaVinci Resolve. Using both is not indecision; it is matching the tool to the stage.

## Verdict

If you publish short-form video regularly, CapCut earns a permanent place in the stack. Confirm the current feature set and any pricing on the official site before relying on a specific feature — this review intentionally lists no price, because prices and plans change.
