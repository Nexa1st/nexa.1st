## Why it is in our workflow

Every cinematic gaming series starts as captured footage, and OBS Studio is the capture tool that costs nothing and asks for nothing. Set up scenes once — game capture, overlay, microphone — and every recording session starts from the same reliable layout.

## Honest limits

OBS records and streams; it does not edit. Beginners also face a first-session learning curve with bitrates and encoders. Both are acceptable trades for software this capable at no cost.

## Verdict

Download it only from obsproject.com, spend one session tuning it to your machine, and it will quietly power years of recordings.
